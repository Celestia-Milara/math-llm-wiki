# 常用空间图形

(1)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_001.jpg)

$$
z = \sqrt {a ^ {2} - x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}}, a > 0
$$

(2)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_002.jpg)

$$
\frac {x}{a} + \frac {y}{b} + \frac {z}{c} = 1, a, b, c > 0,
$$

$$
x, y, z \geqslant 0
$$

(3)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_003.jpg)

$$
z = \sqrt {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}
$$

(4)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_004.jpg)

$$
x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = z ^ {2}
$$

(5)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_005.jpg)

$$
z = x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}
$$

(6)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_006.jpg)

$$
x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = a ^ {2}, z \geqslant 0, a > 0
$$

(7)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_007.jpg)

$$
\frac {x ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}} + \frac {y ^ {2}}{b ^ {2}} - \frac {z ^ {2}}{c ^ {2}} = 1
$$

(8)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_008.jpg)

$$
\frac {x ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}} - \frac {y ^ {2}}{b ^ {2}} - \frac {z ^ {2}}{c ^ {2}} = 1
$$

(9)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_009.jpg)

$$
\sqrt {x} + \sqrt {y} + \sqrt {z} = \sqrt {a}, a > 0
$$

(10)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_010.jpg)

$$
z = x y
$$

(11)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_011.jpg)

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} z = x y, \\ y = x, \\ x = 1, \\ z = 0 \end{array} \right.
$$

(12)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_012.jpg)

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} z = x y, \\ x + y = 1, \\ z = 0 \end{array} \right.
$$

(13)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_013.jpg)

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} z = x y, \\ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = a ^ {2} (a > 0) \end{array} \right.
$$

(14)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_014.jpg)

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} z = x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}, \\ z = 1 - x ^ {2} \end{array} \right.
$$

(15)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_015.jpg)

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 1, \\ z = 1 - x ^ {2} \end{array} \right.
$$

(17)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_016.jpg)

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} z = x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}, \\ x ^ {2} + (y - 1) ^ {2} = 1 \end{array} \right.
$$

(16)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_017.jpg)

$$
x ^ {2} + (y - z) ^ {2} = (1 - z) ^ {2}, 0 \leqslant z \leqslant 1
$$

(18)

![](images/images/21_附录3_常用空间图形_018.jpg)

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} z = 2 \left(x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}\right), \\ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = x, \\ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 2 x, \\ z = 0 \end{array} \right.
$$
